# Maintainers

| Maintainer | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| [@saurav-samantray](https://github.com/saurav-samantray) | Project stewardship, reviews, releases, connector ownership and private security triage |

Connector owners are listed on each connector's page under [docs/connectors](docs/connectors) and
in `.github/CODEOWNERS`.

Maintainers enforce the contribution, connector, security, compatibility and release policies in
this repository. New maintainers are nominated based on sustained, trusted contributions and
documented through a reviewed update to this file, `GOVERNANCE.md` and `.github/CODEOWNERS`.

Security reports must use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a maintainer's
personal contact details.
